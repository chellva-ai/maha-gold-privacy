Privacy Policy

Maha Gold App (மஹா கோல்டு) — Last updated: September 10, 2026

MSK Maha Gold Private Limited ("we", "our", "us") operates the Maha Gold App (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Phone Number: When you sign in, we use Firebase Phone Authentication to verify your mobile number via a one-time password (OTP). We use this number to identify you and, where applicable, to match branch/admin staff accounts.
  • Lead / Enquiry Information: If you submit an enquiry about a gold loan, we collect details you provide such as your name, phone number, and loan-related preferences, so our branch staff can contact you.
  • Device and Usage Data: Basic technical information such as device type and app version may be collected automatically to help us maintain and improve the App.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, camera roll, or messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and provide secure access to the App
  • To respond to gold loan enquiries and connect you with the relevant branch
  • To route calls and WhatsApp messages you choose to initiate to our branch/customer service numbers
  • To maintain, operate, and improve the App

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify you:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for phone number authentication (OTP verification). Google's Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Firebase: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services: Required for standard Android app functionality.
  • WhatsApp: If you tap the WhatsApp button, you will be redirected to the WhatsApp application, which operates under its own privacy policy.
  • Phone Dialer: If you tap the Call button, the App opens your device's default phone dialer to place a call. We do not record or store this call.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your enquiry and contact details are shared only internally within MSK Maha Gold Private Limited (e.g. with the relevant branch office) to respond to your gold loan enquiry.

5. Data Retention

We retain your phone number and enquiry information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements applicable to gold loan finance companies in India.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law and our legitimate business/regulatory requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
