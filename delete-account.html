Delete Your Account or Data

Maha Gold App (மஹா கோல்டு) by MSK Maha Gold Private Limited — Last updated: September 19, 2026

This page explains how you can ask MSK Maha Gold Private Limited to delete your account and personal data collected through the Maha Gold App (மஹா கோல்டு).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to mskfinance7@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. In the email, mention the mobile number you used to sign in to the App, and your name.
  3. Tell us whether you want all your data deleted (account deletion) or only some of it (for example, only your enquiry details).
  4. We will confirm your identity using the mobile number and reply to you by email once your request has been processed.

Data that is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (mobile number) in the App's authentication service
  • Your name and city, and any gold loan enquiry details you submitted through the App

Data that may be kept

  • Records we are required to keep under applicable law and regulations for gold loan finance companies in India (for example, if you have become a loan customer at one of our branches). These records are kept only for the period the law requires and are not used for any other purpose.
